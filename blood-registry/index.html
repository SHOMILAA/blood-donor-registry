📝 Eligibility Requirements:

  • ✅ You must be at least 18 years old and in good health.
  • ✅ You should weigh a minimum of 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • ✅ You should not have donated blood in the last 3 months.
  • ✅ You must be free from infections and not under any restricted medication.
  • ✅ You must have slept well and had a light meal before donation.
